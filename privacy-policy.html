Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This privacy notice describes how the application Amazon Dashboard ("the Application") handles information. The Application is a private, internal-use desktop tool operated by its owner ("we", "us") solely for the owner's own business analytics. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the owner.

1. Information We Access

The Application accesses advertising data (such as impressions, clicks, advertising cost, and advertised product identifiers) from the owner's own Amazon advertising account via the Amazon Advertising API, strictly under the advertising::campaign_management scope, after the owner grants explicit consent through Login with Amazon.

2. How We Use the Information

The data is used exclusively to compute per-ASIN sales, advertising spend, and profit metrics for the owner's own internal business analysis. The data is not used for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage

All retrieved data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. No data is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by us or by any third party.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose any information obtained through the Amazon Advertising API to any third party, except as required by law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only on the owner's local machine and can be permanently deleted at any time by the owner by removing the Application's local data files. No copies exist elsewhere.

6. Third-Party Services

The Application communicates only with Amazon's official API endpoints (Login with Amazon and the Amazon Advertising API). No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are embedded in the Application.

7. Changes to This Notice

If this notice is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated revision date.
